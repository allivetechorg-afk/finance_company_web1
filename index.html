Who We Are

Owned by Our Members, Built for Cameroon's Economic Growth.

Founded in Douala in 2008, Cameroon Unity Cooperative Credit Union (CUCCU) operates on the democratic cooperative principle of "One Member, One Vote." Unlike traditional commercial banks, our surpluses are returned directly to members as annual dividends and lower loan interest rates.

We bridge the gap between traditional Cameroonian solidarity savings (Njangi / Tontines) and modern digital microfinance, ensuring full transparency under CEMAC / COBAC Category 1 microfinance regulations.

How to Become a CUCCU Member in 3 Simple Steps

Membership is open to all Cameroonian citizens, residents, registered Njangi groups, and local enterprises. Become a co-owner in less than 15 minutes.

01

Submit Online Pre-Registration or Visit Any Branch

Complete our digital pre-registration form online or walk into any CUCCU branch in Douala, Yaoundé, Bafoussam, Bamenda, or Buea.

02

Provide KYC Identification Documents

Present a valid Cameroon National Identity Card (CNI) or Passport (or valid receipt + birth certificate), 2 passport-size photos, and a localization sketch (Plan de localisation).

03

Subscribe to Cooperative Shares & Initial Deposit

Pay your one-time registration fee and subscribe to your member shares via cash, MTN MoMo, or Orange Money to receive your Passbook & Member Number immediately.
